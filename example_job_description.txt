NOC Technician

Company: Example Networks
Location: San Francisco, CA
Employment Type: Full-time

Job Summary:
Example Networks is seeking an entry-level NOC Technician to monitor network systems, respond to alerts, troubleshoot connectivity issues, and escalate incidents when necessary.

Responsibilities:
- Monitor network devices and service dashboards.
- Investigate connectivity and performance alerts.
- Perform basic troubleshooting for TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, and VLAN issues.
- Document incidents and troubleshooting steps.
- Escalate unresolved problems to senior engineers.
- Communicate status updates to internal teams.

Required Qualifications:
- Associate or bachelor's degree in information technology, computer engineering, computer science, or a related field.
- Basic understanding of TCP/IP networking.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to work occasional evening or weekend shifts.

Preferred Qualifications:
- CompTIA Network+ or CCNA.
- Experience with Linux.
- Familiarity with ticketing systems and network monitoring tools.
- Previous technical support or laboratory experience.
